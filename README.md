# Aqua-System

Aqua-System is a student team's proposal for the Challenge Cup competition. We are exploring how a mobile robot could map soil moisture across a field and help growers decide where to irrigate and how much water to apply. The project is in the design stage.

### How a field survey would work

The robot would stop at sampling points along a route, insert a probe into the soil, and upload each reading with its location. The proposed AI decision system would use these measurements to identify areas at risk of water shortage and estimate their irrigation needs. Growers could view the route, moisture map, and irrigation recommendations together on a field map.

### Robot designs under consideration

- **Soil moisture survey.** A four-wheeled robot would visit several sampling points on each trip to map how soil moisture varies across the field.
- **Camera-assisted survey.** A camera would record signs such as leaf wilting. Soil readings would help interpret those observations, since heat or disease can also cause wilting.
- **Targeted irrigation.** A robot carrying a tank and pump would water selected areas, then measure soil moisture again to assess the effect and adjust the next application.

## 智墒巡田

智墒巡田是面向挑战杯的学生团队项目，目前处于方案设计阶段。我们希望用移动机器人测出同一块田里不同位置的土壤水分差异，为“哪里需要浇水、需要浇多少”提供依据。

### 一次巡田如何进行

机器人沿路线到达各个采样点，将探针插入土壤，记录并上传读数和位置。拟设计的 AI 决策系统将根据多点数据识别缺水风险较高的区域，估算灌溉需求。种植者可以在平台的农田地图上对照巡田路线、墒情分布和各区域的灌溉建议。

### 正在比较的三种机器人方案

- **移动式墒情监测。** 四轮小车每次巡田测量多个点位，用采样结果绘制农田墒情分布图。
- **视觉辅助巡田。** 在小车上加装摄像头，记录叶片萎蔫等现象，再结合土壤读数判断缺水风险。高温或病害也可能引起萎蔫。
- **局部精准灌溉。** 小车搭载水箱和水泵，对选定区域补水，随后复测土壤水分，用复测结果调整下一次灌水量。
